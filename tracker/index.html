PrimeRiseTech Tracker

Track work time, review timesheets, and manage team leave in your company’s workspace.

Download for Windows

64-bit Windows installer · Requires installation by an administrator

Company administrators

  1. Create your company workspace and save its address.
  2. Download and install Tracker. Enter your workspace address when prompted.
  3. Sign in and use Team → Invite employee to invite staff.
Create company workspace

Employees

  1. Open the invitation from your company administrator and create your employee account.
  2. Download and install Tracker. Use the workspace address shown in the invitation page.
  3. Sign in with the email and password for that company.
